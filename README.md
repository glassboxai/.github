# The Glass Box AI Project

### AI YOU CAN UNDERSTAND.

**The Glass Box AI Project** is an independent, open-source research and development initiative focused on building understandable, inspectable, and trustworthy Artificial Intelligence.

Our mission is to move beyond Black Box AI by exploring transparent architectures, explainable reasoning, human oversight, and modular AI systems.

## Our Projects

- **NeXis** — An experimental modular AI architecture designed to explore transparent and trustworthy intelligence.
- **aXiom** — Knowledge activation and management.
- **veXtor** — Semantic processing and information flow.
- **fliXer** — Logical reasoning and logic flow.
- **μNeX** — Adaptive intelligence.
- **AgentiX** — Human-in-the-Loop agentic software development for software engineers.

## Our Philosophy

We believe that AI should not only be powerful, but also understandable, inspectable, and accountable.

Our work emphasizes open research, modularity, reproducibility, and community-driven development.

**AI is OK, but not this way.**
