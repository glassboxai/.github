**The Glass Box AI Project** is an independent, open-source Artificial Intelligence research and development initiative exploring **Glass Box AI, Explainable AI (XAI), Trustworthy AI, AI Safety, Sovereign AI, and modular AI architectures**.

The goal is to move beyond opaque Black Box AI systems by investigating approaches to AI that are more **understandable, inspectable, traceable, auditable, and controllable by humans**.

Research and development are conducted on experimental software architectures that combine knowledge processing, semantic representations, logical reasoning, adaptive intelligence, and human oversight.

**AI is OK, but not this way.**

## NeXis — A Modular AI Architecture

**NeXis** is an experimental, modular Artificial Intelligence architecture developed within The Glass Box AI Project.

Rather than relying exclusively on opaque, monolithic AI models, NeXis explores how specialized and independently inspectable components can work together to support more transparent and trustworthy AI systems.

Its research encompasses two complementary directions:

- **αNeX** (LLM Control and Verification): Exploring mechanisms to improve the reliability, traceability, and controllability of Large Language Models.
- **μNeX** (Adaptive Intelligence): Investigating alternative approaches to adaptive intelligence through modular computational structures.

## Open-Source Projects

### αNeX — LLM Control and Verification

It consists of the following modules:

- #### aXiom — Knowledge Processing, Activation and Retrieval

**aXiom** explores mechanisms for knowledge representation, activation, and controlled access to information in AI systems.

Its research focuses on making knowledge processing more structured, inspectable, and traceable, aiming to extract only evidence-based information.

- #### veXtor — Semantic Information Flow

**veXtor** explores semantic information processing and the management of semantic relationships within modular AI architectures.

The project investigates how semantic representations and information flows can contribute to guiding the LLM generative process to obtain more concise answers.

- #### fliXer — Logical Processing and Reasoning

**fliXer** investigates structured logical processing and reasoning mechanisms for modular AI systems.

Its goal is to explore how explicit logical operations can complement statistical AI approaches and support more understandable reasoning processes.

### μNeX — Adaptive Intelligence Research

**μNeX** explores adaptive intelligence architectures and alternative computational approaches to AI beyond conventional monolithic neural network systems.

### [AgentiX](https://github.com/glassboxai/AgentiX) — Human-in-the-Loop Agentic Software Engineering

**AgentiX** is an open-source, AI-powered software development system designed specifically for software engineers and professional developers.

It explores a **Human-in-the-Loop approach to agentic software development**, combining AI-assisted code generation with software architecture, modular reuse, systematic testing, developer supervision, and software quality assurance.

AgentiX emphasizes **Specification-Driven Development (SDD), Test-Driven Development (TDD), maintainability, security, and technical debt reduction**.

Its central principle is that AI can generate software, but qualified software developers must remain responsible for understanding, validating, and controlling the resulting systems.

## Research Principles

All this work is guided by several fundamental principles:

- **Transparency:** AI behavior should be understandable and inspectable.
- **Traceability:** Decisions and information flows should be open to examination.
- **Modularity:** Complex AI systems should be decomposable into specialized components.
- **Human Oversight:** Humans must retain meaningful control over AI systems.
- **Reproducibility:** Research, experiments, and results should be documented and verifiable.
- **Open Source:** Research and development should encourage collaboration, independent evaluation, and community-driven improvement.
